# Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping

Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express domestic shipping
takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 8.99 GBP. Orders over 50 GBP qualify for
free standard shipping.

Orders placed after 14:00 on a business day are dispatched the following
business day.

## International shipping

International shipping takes 7 to 21 business days depending on destination and
customs processing. The customer is responsible for any import duties levied by
the destination country.

We do not ship lithium batteries by air. Orders containing them are routed by
surface freight, which adds approximately 10 business days.

## Lost and damaged parcels

A parcel is considered lost 15 business days after its last tracking scan for
domestic orders, and 30 business days for international orders. Claims for
damaged goods must be filed within 7 days of delivery with photographic
evidence.
