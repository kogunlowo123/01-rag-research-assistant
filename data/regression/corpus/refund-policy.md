# Refund Policy

Version 4.2. Effective 1 March 2026. Supersedes version 4.1.

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Proof of purchase is
required for every refund request submitted through the self-service portal.

Digital goods are eligible for a refund within 14 days of purchase, provided the
download count is zero. Once a digital item has been downloaded it is no longer
refundable, because the licence has been consumed.

## Exclusions

The following are not refundable under any circumstances:

- Gift cards and store credit
- Personalised or custom-manufactured items
- Perishable goods
- Services already delivered in full

## Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval. The amount is
returned to the original payment method. Where the original method has expired,
the refund is issued as store credit and the customer is notified by email.

Refunds above 500 GBP require approval from a finance manager before processing.

## Escalation

A customer who disputes a refund decision may escalate to the customer advocacy
team within 60 days of the decision. Escalations are acknowledged within two
business days.
